Coloured Render

Coloured Render in Brynmawr and across Gwent

Coloured render means the colour is in the material, not painted onto it. It goes on wet in the shade you have chosen and it stays that shade, so there is no repainting every few years and nothing to flake off a south facing wall.

The range is wide, from off whites and creams through to greys and darker heritage colours. We have done houses, shop fronts and commercial units in it, and the frontage is usually the thing the owner is most pleased with afterwards.

Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Colour itself does not usually change the price much. What changes it is the size of the elevations, the access, and what is on the wall already.

Free site survey, no call-out charge, and a written price for the whole job before anything starts.

We survey for free and price the whole job in writing, including the scaffold and any preparation. Darker colours and specialist shades can carry a small premium from the manufacturer, and if yours does we will tell you that at quote stage rather than adding it later.

How The Job Runs

What to Expect

  1. Free site surveyWe measure the elevations and check the substrate.
  2. Pick your colour, then see itThis is the part people find most useful. We send you a virtual image of your own property in the shade you are considering. A colour that looks right on a card can look completely different across a whole gable.
  3. PrepScaffold, fittings off, existing surface made sound.
  4. Beads and basecoatBeads set true, basecoat and mesh applied.
  5. Colour coatThe through coloured finish goes on and is floated to the texture you have picked.
  6. Clean upScaffold down, everything back on, site left clean.
