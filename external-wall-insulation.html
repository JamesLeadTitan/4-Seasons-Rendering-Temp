External Wall Insulation

External Wall Insulation in Brynmawr and across Gwent

Most houses round here were built with solid walls and no cavity to fill. That is why they are cold, why the heating runs all evening and why the back bedroom never quite warms up. External wall insulation is the fix. We fix insulation board to the outside of the house, reinforce it, and finish it in render, so the walls hold heat in instead of letting it straight out through the stone.

It is the biggest single improvement you can make to an old solid wall property, and it changes how the house looks at the same time. You get a warmer home and a front elevation that looks new, out of one job.

How The Job Runs

What to Expect

  1. Free site surveyWe come out, measure up and look at the walls. We check the substrate, the ground levels, the eaves and where the scaffold has to stand.
  2. You see it before we startWe send you a virtual image of your own property finished in the colour you are thinking about, so you are not picking off a sample card.
  3. Scaffold and prepScaffold goes up, downpipes and any fittings come off, and the base track is set level all the way round.
  4. Boards and carrier boardInsulation board is fixed and mechanically fixed back to the wall. Where the substrate is timber frame or will not take render directly, a cement carrier board goes on first to give the system something solid to work off.
  5. Beads, mesh and basecoatCorner beads and bellcast go on, then the basecoat with the reinforcing mesh bedded into it. This is the layer that stops cracking later.
  6. Finish and clean upPrimer, then the finish coat in your colour. Scaffold comes down, fittings go back on and we clear the site.
