Pebble Dashing

Pebble Dashing in Brynmawr and across Gwent

Half the houses in the valleys are dashed, and most of that dash has been on a long time. We do three things with it. We put fresh dash on where that is the finish you want, we patch and match existing dash where a section has come away, and we take it off and replace it with a modern render where you have had enough of it.

There is no right answer between those. It depends on the house, the street and what you want it to look like in ten years.

Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Patching is priced by the area and how well it has to blend. A full re-dash is priced like any other render job, on elevations and access. Removing old dash is the one that varies most, because you do not know what is behind it until it comes off.

Free site survey, no call-out charge, and a written price for the whole job before anything starts.

The survey is free and we will tell you honestly which of the three we would do on your house. Where we are taking old dash off, the quote covers making good what is underneath, so you are not left with a bill for a wall we uncovered.

How The Job Runs

What to Expect

  1. Free site surveyWe check how sound the existing dash is by tapping it. Hollow areas are the ones that come away.
  2. Decide the approachPatch, re-dash or remove and render. We give you our honest view and the price for each where it is worth comparing.
  3. PrepScaffold up, loose material off, edges cut back to something solid.
  4. BasecoatThe receiving coat goes on, ready to take the stone while it is still green.
  5. Throw the dashThe stone is thrown onto the wet coat by hand and pressed in. Matching existing work means matching the stone size and colour as well as the coverage.
  6. Clean upLoose stone swept, scaffold down, site cleared.
