Rendering and Wall Insulation in Abergavenny

Abergavenny

Local Rendering and Wall Insulation You Can Trust

Abergavenny has a different housing stock to the valley towns and it needs handling differently. There is a lot of period property, plenty of stone, and enough of it sits in or near conservation areas that what you put on the wall is not purely your own choice. Slapping a modern render on an old stone elevation is how you cause damp.

So the work here leans toward breathable systems, careful colour choices that sit right in a market town rather than shouting, and being straight with people about when the answer is to leave the stone alone.

What We Do in Abergavenny

Breathable render on period and stone property, sympathetic colour work, and modern systems on the newer housing around the town. We cover the market town, the period stock and the outlying villages, and we are close enough to come out and look at the job rather than pricing it off a photograph you send us.
