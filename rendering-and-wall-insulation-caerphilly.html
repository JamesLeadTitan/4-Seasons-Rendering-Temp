Caerphilly

Local Rendering and Wall Insulation You Can Trust

Caerphilly has more pebble dash per street than almost anywhere we work, and most of it has been on since long before the current owners moved in. Some of it is still sound and worth patching. A lot of it is drumming hollow when you tap it, which means water has got behind the basecoat and pushed it off the wall.

That is the conversation we end up having on most Caerphilly jobs. Patch it, re-dash it, or take it off and put a modern render on. We will tell you which one your house actually needs, including when the answer is the cheap one.

What We Do in Caerphilly

Dash repair and matching, full re-dash, and taking old dash off in favour of silicone or monocouche. We cover the terraces, the estates and the streets around the castle, and we are close enough to come out and look at the job rather than pricing it off a photograph you send us.
