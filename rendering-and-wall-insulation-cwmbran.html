Rendering and Wall Insulation in Cwmbran

Cwmbran

Local Rendering and Wall Insulation You Can Trust

Cwmbran is a new town, and that tells you almost everything about the render work here. A great deal of the housing went up between the fifties and the seventies, and a lot of it is system built or no fines concrete. Those walls are cold, they are hard to insulate from the inside without losing the room, and they are exactly what external wall insulation was designed for.

We have done enough of this stock to know what is behind the finish before we open it up. Where the substrate will not take render directly we board it first, which is a step plenty of firms skip and then blame the material when it cracks.

What We Do in Cwmbran

External wall insulation on the system built stock, full render systems over carrier board, and modern finishes replacing tired dash. We cover the new town stock and everything built around it, and we are close enough to come out and look at the job rather than pricing it off a photograph you send us.
