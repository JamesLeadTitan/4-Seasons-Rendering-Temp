Rendering and Wall Insulation in Risca

Risca

Local Rendering and Wall Insulation You Can Trust

Risca runs along the floor of the Ebbw valley, and the Victorian terraces along it are the classic solid wall job. Narrow frontages, shared party walls, long rear elevations and no cavity anywhere. The heating bills on those houses are the reason most people ring us in the first place.

Above them the newer estates are a different job entirely, usually a tired finish that wants bringing back rather than a wall that wants insulating. We price both honestly rather than pushing everyone toward the bigger job.

What We Do in Risca

Solid wall insulation on the terraces, re-render and dash removal on the estates above. We cover the valley floor terraces and the estates above them, and we are close enough to come out and look at the job rather than pricing it off a photograph you send us.
