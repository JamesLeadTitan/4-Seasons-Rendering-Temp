Rendering and Wall Insulation in Tredegar

Tredegar

Local Rendering and Wall Insulation You Can Trust

Tredegar is high, old and built mostly of stone, which makes it one of the more interesting places we work. Stone walls behave differently to brick. They hold moisture and they need to be able to let it back out, so the wrong finish traps damp in the wall and causes problems that were not there before.

That is why we are careful about what goes on a Tredegar property. Breathable systems on anything that needs to breathe, and we will say so at the survey rather than selling you whatever we happen to be spraying that month.

What We Do in Tredegar

Breathable render on stone, external wall insulation where the construction suits it, and dash work across the town. We cover the stone terraces and the streets around the town centre, and we are close enough to come out and look at the job rather than pricing it off a photograph you send us.
